# Abdul Urban Style – Digital Marketing Campaign

**Student:** Abdul Aziz  
**Project:** Digital Marketing Campaign  
**Platform:** Angular Ecommerce Website

## Project Overview

Abdul Urban Style is an ecommerce website developed as the foundation for a digital marketing campaign. The project combines an online shopping experience with practical digital marketing strategies designed to increase brand awareness, attract potential customers, and support online sales.

## Campaign Objectives

- Increase online brand awareness
- Drive relevant traffic to the ecommerce website
- Improve visibility through search engines
- Reach potential customers through paid advertising
- Promote products using engaging digital creatives
- Encourage website visits and purchase intent

## SEO Strategy

The SEO campaign focuses on improving the website's organic visibility through:

- Relevant fashion and ecommerce keywords
- Optimized page titles and descriptions
- Product-focused content
- Search-friendly website structure
- Mobile responsive design
- Clear product categories and navigation

## Google Ads Campaign

Google Ads will target users actively searching for fashion and lifestyle products.

**Campaign Type:** Search Campaign  
**Goal:** Website Traffic and Sales  
**Target Audience:** Online shoppers interested in fashion and lifestyle products

Example keywords:

- men's fashion online
- urban style clothing
- affordable fashion
- online clothing store
- trendy fashion products

## Meta Ads Campaign

Meta advertising will be used across Facebook and Instagram to build awareness and attract potential customers.

**Objectives:**
- Brand awareness
- Website traffic
- Product promotion
- Customer engagement

Creative formats include product advertisements, promotional graphics, carousel-style content, and call-to-action messages.

## Website Features

- Ecommerce product catalogue
- Product search
- Category filtering
- Product prices
- Stock availability
- Shopping cart
- Responsive layout
- User-friendly navigation
